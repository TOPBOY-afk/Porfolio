About Me

A curious mind from Ghana 🇬🇭

I'm Kwaku, an 18 year old aspiring developer who fell in love with the web. I was amazed by how everything online works — social media, AI, interactive websites — and I knew I had to learn how to build these things myself.

I started with Python in November last year, and found my way to web development where things really started to click. I'm currently mastering HTML and CSS, with JavaScript and beyond on the horizon.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me playing Free Fire 🎮 — but my big dream? Building my own AI someday.
